All About Chickens

Chickens (Gallus gallus domesticus) are domesticated birds descended from the red junglefowl of Southeast Asia. They are one of the most common and widespread domestic animals in the world, with a total population exceeding 25 billion.

Why People Love Chickens

  • Fresh eggs — a single hen can lay 250+ eggs per year.
  • Pest control — chickens love eating insects, ticks, and weeds.
  • Great fertilizer — chicken manure is excellent for compost.
  • Friendly pets — many hens have big personalities and can even recognize faces.

Fun Chicken Facts

  1. Chickens are the closest living relatives of the Tyrannosaurus rex.
  2. A hen communicates with her chicks before they even hatch, clucking softly through the shell.
  3. Chickens can remember over 100 different faces — of both people and other chickens.
  4. The record for most yolks in one egg is nine!
  5. There are hundreds of chicken breeds, from the tiny Serama to the giant Brahma.

Caring for Chickens

Chickens need a secure coop, fresh water, quality feed, and space to roam and scratch. They are social animals, so they should always be kept in groups of at least three. With good care, a healthy hen can live 8–10 years.
